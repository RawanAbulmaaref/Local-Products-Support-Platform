# Local Products Support Platform

## Project Overview

The Local Products Support Platform is a project that supports local products by connecting the people who sell them with the people who want to buy them. Sellers add their products to the platform, and buyers browse those products and place orders.

## How the Platform Works

- **Sellers** register with their name, email, phone number, and address. Each seller can add as many products as they want, and every product has a name and a price.
- **Buyers** register with their name, email, and phone number. A buyer can place as many orders as they need.
- **Orders** are placed by buyers. Each order has a date, a status, and a total price.
- **Order items** are the products inside an order. A single order can include several products, each with its own quantity and the price it was bought at. The same product can also appear in orders from different buyers.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to give local sellers a place to offer their products and give buyers an easy way to order them, while keeping track of every order from the moment it is placed.
